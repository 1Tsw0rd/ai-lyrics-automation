1. Rain on the Window

The bus goes by, I let it slide
No plans yet, just this quiet chair
Rain on the window, going nowhere

Morning holds me soft and slow

[Chorus]

Gray sky settles, takes its time
I'm not moving, I'm just fine
Rain on the window, keeping time
